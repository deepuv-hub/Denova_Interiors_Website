Cost Planning Accuracy

How Cost Calculations Work

Avoid technical confusion. Discover exactly how modular kitchens, closets, and ceilings are mathematically measured in Bangalore.

Modular Kitchen (R.Ft)

Measured in **Running Feet (R.Ft)** along the horizontal counter length. Separate calculations coordinate the bottom cabinetry framework, countertops, and upper wall lockers.

Counter Length (Ft) × R.Ft Unit Cost

Modular Wardrobes (Sq.Ft)

Measured in **Square Feet (Sq.Ft)** along the wardrobe's front profile area. Calculated strictly by multiplying wardrobe total Height by total Width.

Height (Ft) × Width (Ft) × Sq.Ft Unit Cost

False Ceiling (Sq.Ft)

Measured in flat **Square Feet (Sq.Ft)** along the horizontal ceiling flat area. Custom drop steps coves are calculated separately to coordinate strip wiring lengths.

Ceiling Length (Ft) × Width (Ft) × Sq.Ft Cost

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers to structural interior materials, waterproof grades, and cost calculations.

We highly recommend using Boiling Water Proof (BWP) marine-grade plywood (IS:710 grade) for all modular kitchen under-sink areas, utility boxes, and bottom cabinets. BWR (Boiling Water Resistant) plywood is suitable for upper dry cabinets, but BWP is mandatory for wet zones to prevent swelling and termite decay.

Commercial plywood is Moisture Resistant (MR) but cannot withstand direct water contact; it swells easily when wet. BWP (Boiling Water Proof) plywood uses superior phenol-formaldehyde synthetic resin, allowing it to withstand boiling water for up to 72 hours without delaminating. BWP is essential for kitchens and bathrooms.

For wardrobes, Matte laminates are highly recommended because they are anti-fingerprint, scratch-resistant, and create a calm luxury aesthetic. Gloss finishes (like Acrylic) are best used sparingly on upper kitchen cabinets or narrow hallways to bounce light and make small areas feel spacious.

Modular wardrobes are calculated based on Square Feet (Sq.Ft) area (Height × Width of the wardrobe front). The cost is determined by the carcass core material (BWR vs MDF), sliding vs. hinged mechanisms, and the surface finish (laminate vs. acrylic or glass).

Modular kitchens are calculated in Running Feet (R.Ft) along the length of the counter. Standard calculations separate the bottom counter cabinets, quartz countertops, and upper wall cabinets. Accessories (tandem boxes, pantries) and premium hardware (Hafele, Hettich) are added to this base rate.
